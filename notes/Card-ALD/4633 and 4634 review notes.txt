5804633 AND 5804634 CARD-ALD REVIEW

The Card-ALD drawings use one installed card of each type as a source-bound
example. Each box is seven text rows high. The circuits and used inputs are
drawn from the current page models; an unused input is not a claim that the
physical card lacks that input. A physical pin is shown once at the outside
edge of each example. The 4633 example has 38 observed portions and two
additional database portions shown closed and unrouted. The 4634 example has
54 observed portions and three additional database portions shown closed and
unrouted.

The diagrams distinguish direct same-page net edges from connections inferred
by matching a physical output and input pin on the same installed card. Their
per-page source and model hashes, edge counts, and working pin interpretations
are in 4633 drawing provenance.json and 4634 drawing provenance.json. A
working pin interpretation in the drawing is not by itself a correction to
the published ALD transcription.

All-occurrence inventories and comparison findings are in the corresponding
JSON files in this directory. A comparison outlier is a review candidate, not
an instruction to normalize a literal printed difference. The physical-page
owner corrected AP321 and AP531 4633 AR portions from U55PC to U55AC, and
AP121 and AP131 4634 portions 08 and 12 from U61AF to U61AE. The owner also
confirmed AP561 block AJ as 463425, correcting the earlier 463429 reading.
AP701 prints A on 4634 portion B1; that function label is retained literally.

The SLT Component Circuits manual, figure 87 (printed page 62), identifies
U03AU as AND-Terminate and U03AW as OR-Terminate Inverter with Load. Its
polarity rules explain AP561's OR label with wedges on both inputs and the
output as an AND circuit in positive logic. AP561 also has a separate
portion 30 labeled U03AW and ✲A. Fourteen other even-bit AP sheets put the
matching OR/U03AU block at portion 25, while all fifteen even-bit sheets put
the ✲A/U03AW block at portion 30. The physical-page confirmation establishes
AP561 AJ as portion 25. Its U03AU circuit and decorations remain unchanged.

The same manual's wedge definition and its U03AM AND-Inverter circuit show
that AP701's A with two plain inputs and a wedged output is the same NAND
function as the other 4634B1 blocks drawn OR with two wedged inputs and a
plain output. This is an equivalent polarity notation, not a function-name
transcription error. These interpretations do not overwrite the printed
functions or wedges in the page text.

The physical-page owner confirmed AP411 4633J4 as U03AM, AP511 4633H1 as
U55AC, AP621 4633G1 as U55AC, AP211 4634 portion 29 as U03AW, and the
4633A7 inputs on AP071 and AP551 as B13. Native review also identified B13
on AP111. Current audit records in this directory bind the prior and corrected
text, data, notes, preview, and original TIFF. Original damaged-glyph
descriptions in historical review records remain available as provenance.

Native scan and matching-portion review corrected 37 further 4633 physical
pins and 57 4634 physical pins. AP491's omitted 463439-to-463452 wire and
the separate 463440-to-463452 middle input were restored in text and data.
AP511's apparent missing join was already recorded as a multi-driver net;
AP391 and AP091 show genuine alternate or separate routes.
The AP701 4634B1 upper input was a transcription mismatch: the native scan
prints G10 above the separate G12 lower input. The page text and net model
were corrected with a source-bound audit.
AP571's 4634 portion 21 output is pinless and feeds portion 29. Its earlier
B08 label was erroneous; B08 belongs only to portion 29's output. The page
text, pin data and driver reference were corrected against the native scan.
Of the remaining repeated-edge comparison entries, 20 join the same
physical pin on an installed 4633 card and are represented as inferred
Card-ALD links. The AP091 4633 entry has separate G03/G04 contacts, and
the AP391 4634 entry is the verified alternate route. Neither indicates a
missing page wire.

The remaining raw invalid-pin flags are not unresolved physical-pin readings.
The 4633 report has 19 internal labels, 15 open-asterisk decorations, and six
logical A/B input designators. The 4634 report has 99 leading border/wire
marks, 25 internal labels, two decorations, and one logical A designator.
Some leading marks remain embedded in the structured transcription and are
interpreted separately when drawing the card; they should not be treated as
letters in a physical pin number. Every named correction above has a current
source-bound acceptance audit.
