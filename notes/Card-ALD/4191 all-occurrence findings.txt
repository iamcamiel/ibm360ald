4191 PRE-CORRECTION ALL-OCCURRENCE FINDINGS (2026-10-15)

Historical findings from before the audited 45-page release. The current
published comparison is in 4191 all-occurrence comparison.json: 644 blocks,
60 adder slices, zero field/pin flags and zero topology disagreements.

Scope: current published AP ALD text and structured JSON, compared with the
02B-E1 K6/K7 Card-ALD example. This is an audit, not an acceptance of changed
source readings. The detailed block and wiring comparisons are in the adjacent
CSV files.

644 blocks on 68 pages use card type 4191. All 45 portion codes occur. On each
page the published text and JSON contain the same multiset of 4191 portions.

Sixty adder slices have a 4191 A-to-OR join. The current structured JSON
links all five A drivers on 53 sheets; it omits two links on AP521 and one
link each on AP541, AP551, AP561, AP571, AP581, and AP591. Direct human
review of those seven physical sheets confirms five separate A-output wires
on EVERY one. AP441 and AP531 also have separate wires, although their JSON
combines a pair of driver outputs into one net. These are nine transcription-
data wiring corrections, not missing or joined wires on the original sheets.

The updated comparison generated overlapping candidate counts: 13 circuit-
identifier differences, one primary-function difference, 49 invalid physical-
pin-like readings, and 11 other physical pin differences. The initial audit
also flagged 95 blocks for one or more K6/K7 example pins not shown (118
individual absences in the current comparison). That category is NOT a
discrepancy: direct human clarification on 2026-10-15 established that an ALD
block can omit inputs that the card portion has but that this use leaves idle.
The card-level input set must be the union of verified uses, not the inputs
visible in each individual block. Other pin differences need native review
before a K6/K7 pin is imposed on another page.

Pin-reading triage now has 58 individual invalid-label observations. Native
and connected-pin review resolves the feedback cases and the D12 contact
pattern; 36 more have a unique same-number contact elsewhere. AP481's
remaining U12 resolves to D12 because its input shares a net with BF.D12
and the same AW box already has a separate J12 input. Physical-page review on
2026-10-15 settled portion 25's disputed contact as D08. AP071's published
J08 and five other U08-like readings on that portion are staged as D08;
AP191 already reads D08. Eleven other physical-pin differences remain in
the published-source comparison, which does not apply staged corrections.
The 36 unique same-number contacts have now been recorded as inferred
corrections and staged as private TXT/JSON candidates. Their peer-page
evidence is in the decision ledger. No invalid-label pin candidate remains
unresolved in the current 4191 triage.
