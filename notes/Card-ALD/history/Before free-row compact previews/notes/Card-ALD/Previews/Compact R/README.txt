PROPOSED COMPACT R PREVIEWS

These files are previews, not replacements for Card-ALD.
All four use their current published layouts.
Shared-net R portions use short dangling labels without pin labels.
Standalone R portions keep standard ALD boxes.
Three extra characters are reserved for function variants.
Input triangles are retained at the endpoint in decorated variants.
All numbered TXT variants are in Numbered variants.
0141 and 4633 have no R portions and are unchanged.
