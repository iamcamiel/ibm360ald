Printed references and portions are reproduced without inferring electrical direction.
E2/E3, E4/E5 and F4/F5 are DOUBLE CARD entries; PLUG LIST counts each card at its first socket.
L4, M2, M3, M4, M5, N2, N3, N4 and N5 have separate CABL and CONNECTOR entries. The connector part numbers are blank in the printed PLUG LIST.
D2 and D3 both print RF701 portions D2 and E1; source overlap retained.
A5, C4, E4, G5, J2, L2, M3 and T2 continue in the next printed column.
K6 omits D1 and explicitly lists D unused.
T3 and T4 pin references ending 6 are printed, not normalized to 4.
