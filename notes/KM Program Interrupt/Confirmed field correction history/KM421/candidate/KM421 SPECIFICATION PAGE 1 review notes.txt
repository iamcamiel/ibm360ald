KM421 SPECIFICATION PAGE 1 — native source review

The 49 printed blocks comprise 48 card bearing gates plus the CJ7G DOT combiner; all 48 card portions on the sheet were compared with the A1 listing. The sheet has 85 printed input terminals. The native top, middle and bottom regions, footer table, and full printer grid rendering were compared visually.

The upper returns were traced from enlarged native crops. AZ.B02 drives AN.B02; its paired D05 wire continues to KN241 and crosses the CP return without a join. BG.B10 drives CP.D11 through the row 11 bridge. BA.D06 continues to KN261 at row 10. AA.B10 drives AN.D04; AJ.D13 drives BF.D13 on a separate row 15 return. BB.D12 crosses the AD.D12 diamond without joining it and continues to AR.B09. The printed KN281 export is on row 13.

The lower controls were checked against independent source crops. FLOATING POINT RX DP feeds AF.B04 and the AA/CH branches; STM feeds AG.B12 and AH.D05; ENABLE feeds AT.B07, AU.B03 and AV.D05; F15 feeds BD.D09. The adjacent D21, D22 and ENABLE verticals remain separate. AG has six printed input pins and AH has seven, including their top and bottom edge pins. The CJ DOT block has no printed card portion, so no card was inferred.
