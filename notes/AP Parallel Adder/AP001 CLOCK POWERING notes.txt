AP001 CLOCK POWERING - REVIEW NOTES

All 42 circuit blocks and all printed labels were visually inspected. Card types,
portions and physical placements are corroborated by the A1 socket listings.
The wiring has 47 nets and 55 input ports. Every input has one output owner;
paths on different nets have no shared line segments or joined diamonds.
Unmarked crossings remain separate. Diamonds represent printed junctions.
Two close vertical routes near BT/BN are kept in separate character columns.

SOURCE INCONSISTENCIES
The footer references flow down the left column, then down the right column.
Thus the top-right 02B-E2D1E11 continues BV4 from the bottom-left.
Some printed footer identifiers differ from the independently transcribed A1:
AG4 at 02B-E1 H8 A06 is AC4 in A1.
BN4 at 02B-D1 N4 D04 is BV4 in A1.
BW4 at 02B-E1 F8 C04 and 02B-E2 F1 C09 is BU4 in A1.
The diagram preserves the printed identifiers. These differences are not
silently reconciled. A1 confirms card portions and connector references;
local gate pin numbers and wiring were read from the diagram itself.
