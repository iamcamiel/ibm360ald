IBM 360 ALD TRANSCRIPTIONS

ALD contains reviewed UTF-8 text transcriptions. Its subdirectories use the
exact directory names of the original IBM_360_ALD reference collection.
Notes contains page review notes, uncertainties, literal source/reference
conflicts, and original scan-order neighbours in the same directory structure.

View the diagrams using JuliaMono or another font that gives every printed
symbol one character cell. Disable word wrapping and retain trailing spaces.
Ordinary ALD circuit pages use 245 columns and 103 rows. Socket listings,
indices, CAS diagrams and ROS listings may use different original page sizes.

Unreadable printed characters are represented by �. Suggested readings and
reference-based reconstructions are recorded in notes rather than silently
replacing damaged print. Handwritten additions are excluded. Printed source
wording, pin labels and socket discrepancies are retained and documented.

For ALD wiring, hollow ◇ marks a printed junction; a plain ┼ is a crossing
without a junction. Some printed straps have no visible external driver.
Uncertain wire continuity and electrical direction remain explicit in notes.
Bank shifts are page-specific and require direct human confirmation.

This repository is a reviewed snapshot of ongoing conversion work. An empty
folder does not mean its reference scans are missing. Drafts awaiting review
remain in the working collection and are added after acceptance. Empty-folder
.gitkeep files preserve the directory structure in Git.

A text filename may preserve literal title symbols where the scan catalogue
uses descriptive words. Page notes identify the exact corresponding TIFF and
its original scan filename. Transcription bytes are copied without reflow,
whitespace trimming, glyph replacement or newline conversion. The repository
.gitattributes entry protects text line endings from automatic Git conversion.

The original TIFF files, detailed machine-readable models, conversion scripts,
and full visual evidence remain outside these ALD and notes folders for now.
