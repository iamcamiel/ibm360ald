CAS transcription review notes

104 UTF-8 CAS text files. All pages have completed visual and wiring review.

Character conventions: ✲ open asterisk; ─ │ ┌ ┐ └ ┘ ┼ for diagram rules; ASCII - in control fields; � for unreadable characters.
A genuine ? is a printed CAS operator. Proposed readings remain in the verification notes.
JuliaMono has every approved character at the same advance width, including the unreadable-character marker.
QZ listings support the address and branch checks. The scans include later revisions, so a difference from QZ does not automatically mean a transcription error.
Symbolic control fields were read from the scans. QZ numeric control bits are included in each page verification file; complete independent numeric encoding of every symbolic field is not asserted.
Rows and columns in Unreadable characters.csv count monospaced character cells from 1.
The before/after files are from preserved original scan timestamps, including scans outside the CAS subset.
