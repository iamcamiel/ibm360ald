## Specific review conclusions

1. **`A OR`:** The manual is conclusive: `A` is primary and `OR` is a DOT-output suffix. The same parsing applies to `OR A` and `N OR` (pp. 17-18 / PDF 18-19, p. 23 / PDF 24). `OR FL` similarly has primary `OR` with storage-use suffix `FL` (pp. 16, 18 / PDF 17, 19).
2. **User-confirmed shape verdicts:** The manual's polarity-equivalent `A`/`OR` examples support preserving the user's visually consistent cases. Its examples also caution against treating two blocks as the same shape unless input and output wedges, connected pins, and external DOT wiring are all represented. It provides no evidence to overturn the user's `0497A2` judgment. The user's `4633K4` versus `4633K8` correction is a literal card-subportion reading to validate on that scan, not something this manual can establish.
3. **0143 group:** The manual's portion convention supports the relationship of C1-C2-C3. It does not mention card 5800143, DT001, DT011, or the exact inversion/switching arrangement. Retain the user's explanation as human-source provenance pending circuit-specific material.
4. **Other labels:** This manual provides definitions for `A`, `OR`, `N`, `AR`, `TD`, `FL`, `FF`, `PH`, `DOT`, and several suffixes. I did not locate `R T0`, `R T1`, `R T2`, `R T3`, or the specific card numbers `0143`, `4633`, `0497` in its 100-page OCR/search layer; do not infer those meanings from silence. The source is a 1965 edition and may not cover the later cards in the user's binder.
