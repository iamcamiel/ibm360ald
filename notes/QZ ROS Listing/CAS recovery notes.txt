These are reconstructed values, not readings of the torn QZ scan.
The E decoder uses bits 81,21–24. The QZ E column contains only 21–24.
STOP1 in QS051 E6 is L1 and does not set the F field.
P2 is separately marked as a parity reconstruction. After recovery,
both 0536 and 0537 satisfy all four parity checks.
The preceding address is 0535 (no CAS reference); the following address
is 0538, located at QG011 N7.
