CAS RECONCILIATION OF THE 49 FLAGGED ROS WORDS

All 49 requested words have been reviewed. Forty-six corresponding CAS boxes
were available. After corrections, 48 words have complete bits and satisfy
the parity checks (including the unused-word convention).

One word remains for a physical-copy check:
03AA — QZ031-04, source row 23; CAS QT031 E5.
The scan currently reads N=0100, P4=1. The CAS box does not show an active
serial-adder input and cannot settle this N-field reading. The last parity
group and whole-word parity are even. The source values have been retained;
cross_check_ready=false. See 03AA review.png.
Preceding and following words: 03A9 (QT031 A5); 03AB (QT031 C5).

No CAS exists for 0300 or 0535 in the QZ references. Both are EC 713381
unused words: P1=1, every readable remaining ROS bit zero. Their missing
cells were filled from this pattern and are explicitly labelled as inferences.

0E00 is referred to QQ451 C9 by the May listing, but the available QQ451
sheet was revised in June and no longer contains that ROS address. Two faded
zeros were recovered from the QZ scan alone. The word now passes parity,
but its control fields have not been independently confirmed by a CAS box.

0EB2 control fields match the available CAS box. Its next address illustrates
a genuine revision limit: the May listing points to 0F38/0F39, whereas the
June QQ431 diagram draws 0FB9. The old QZ word is preserved.

0AAF belongs to QQ041 E5; the formerly transcribed E9 reference was corrected.

Four unreadable parity bits were inferred after the other group bits were
checked: 0224 P2=1, 0C24 P2=0, 0F24 P2=1, 0F25 P4=1.
These are parity inferences, not CAS symbols or direct scan readings.

An additional issue was caught outside the original 49: 0528 had two false
ones in the N field. Enlarged zeros and the unused-word pattern confirm N0.
