                                                                               THE TAPE DRIVE IS READING THE GAP:
                                                                              1. POSITION THE TAPE SO THAT THE GAP IN QUESTION
                                                                                 IS NEXT.
                                                                              2. SET S AND T AS REQUIRED.
                                                                              3. JUMPER E-B3H6B12 (+FLT COND SUBSYSTEM RESET.
                                                                                 KU291) TO D08 GROUND.

                                                                                 BE ACCOMPLISHED BY QUICKLY TOUCHING E-B3H6D12 TO GND.
